# Add GPT-5.2 Pro model-selection and reliability guidance to docs

## CONTEXT
OpenAI’s ChatGPT release notes describe GPT-5.2 Pro as the “smartest and most trustworthy model yet” for difficult questions where quality is worth waiting for, with early testing showing fewer major errors and stronger performance in complex domains such as programming. Source: https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes

## WHY IT MATTERS
“Most trustworthy” is a high-bar claim. We need supporting guidance that explains ideal use cases, latency/quality tradeoffs, known limitations, and when users should choose Instant, Thinking, or Pro variants.

## RECOMMENDED ACTION
Prepare a docs/release-note PR adding model-selection guidance for GPT-5.2 Instant, Thinking, and Pro, including expected latency tradeoffs, evaluation caveats, examples of complex-domain use, and limitations language.
