## WHY IT MATTERS
Glean is signaling category leadership with rapid enterprise traction, large-customer penetration, and enough capital to invest aggressively in product, partnerships, and international expansion. Our sales and exec teams need the latest validated metrics and likely objection handling for prospects who cite Glean’s momentum.

## RECOMMENDED ACTION
Update the Glean competitive profile/battlecard with the latest ARR, funding, valuation, Fortune 500, international, and feature-velocity claims. Include guidance on how to respond without over-indexing on vanity metrics: emphasize our differentiated customer outcomes, deployment model, governance, ROI, and product strengths.
